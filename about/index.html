About E-HOME

One home brand.
One complete system.

E-HOME is an Australian-market backyard home brand, designed and manufactured by E3 TECH. It brings residential design and building technology together in a home that feels considered, resolved and ready for everyday life.

Behind every E-HOME sits one coordinated system. Design, engineering and production work together from the first conversation through to delivery, so decisions remain clear and the finished home stays true to what was approved.

The company

E3 TECH brings the whole building process under one roof.

E3 TECH designs, engineers and manufactures prefabricated building systems. E-HOME turns that capability into a focused range of backyard homes for Australia.

Instead of treating design, technical resolution and production as separate handovers, our teams develop them as one connected process. The same system carries the original design intent into the structure, envelope, services and interior finish.

This approach combines the quality expected of a permanent residence with the control of industrial production. More work happens in a managed environment before the home reaches site, supporting a faster installation and a more predictable delivery.

The brand

A backyard home designed around the way it will be used.

E-HOME spans one- to multi-bedroom layouts, with considered choices in façades and finishes. The range can support rental investment, independent family living or a quiet place to work, without asking every project to begin from a blank page.

Each home arrives with its structure, weatherproofing, insulation, windows, doors and interior systems coordinated in advance. Following local site works and installation, the home is completed to move-in standard.

One coordinated delivery

100 days.
One system.

Our delivery model connects off-site precision with local installation. Design and engineering are resolved before production, then the home is manufactured, shipped and assembled through one planned sequence.

That sequence supports faster installation and a more predictable delivery date, with a target of 100 days from confirmation to a completed E-HOME. Homes can be delivered internationally, with timing adapted to local approvals, transport and site conditions.
